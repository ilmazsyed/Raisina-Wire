# Rashtrix app — start here

This `rashtrix` branch is the starting point for the Rashtrix app, a Bengali-first rebuild of Raisina Wire.

- Read `rashtrix/BRIEF.md` for every decision and the five build phases.
- Brand files are in `rashtrix/brand/` (logo, breaking-news creative, two YouTube Shorts jacket frames).
- Design mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/CtopbLrBdTaJpNmYv5c4UV
- The Rashtrix app must deploy as its own Cloudflare Worker and D1 database, never over Raisina Wire's.
- Do not merge this branch into `main` (that is the live Raisina Wire site).

First step: begin Phase 1.
